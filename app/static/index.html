💡 How it works

Submit what you paid at a local vendor. The system compares your price against the official National Institute of Statistics (INS) wholesale benchmark and applies a +10% fair-margin to compute the Fair Trade Threshold.


GREEN Price is at or below the fair threshold — good deal.
RED Price exceeds the threshold — vendor is price-gouging.

Formula: Threshold = (INS bulk price ÷ units per bulk) × 1.10
